ℹ️ About MSCE LIBRARY

MSCE LIBRARY is a free resource hub created to help Malawi secondary school students prepare effectively for the Malawi School Certificate of Education (MSCE) examinations administered by MANEB.

We prioritise MSCE subjects, organise past papers by subject (not randomly), and highlight the high-yield topics that examiners return to year after year.

Past papers are sourced from publicly available educational collections such as FiloKin Edu and other free sharing platforms. Always cross-check with your teachers and official MANEB information.

More features (marking schemes, detailed study guides, pathway information for the new curriculum) will be added as we grow. Stay focused and study smart! 💪
