💡 Smart Exam Tips

Practical advice that helps you score higher, not just study longer.

How MSCE is awarded

You need either:

  • Pass in 6 subjects including English, with at least one credit/distinction, or
  • Pass in 5 subjects including English, with at least three credits/distinctions

English is compulsory. Protect that grade.

Study smarter, not longer

  • Master the unmissable topics first — they appear most often
  • Practise timed past papers after learning a topic, not before
  • Review mistakes carefully — that is where real improvement happens
  • Use active recall: close the book and write what you remember

In the exam room

  • Read every question twice before answering
  • Start with questions you know well to build confidence
  • Show clear working in Maths and Sciences — method marks matter
  • Manage time: leave a few minutes to check answers

Subject-specific focus

  • English: Practise summary and composition weekly
  • Maths: Master algebra and graphs early
  • Sciences: Learn to draw and label diagrams accurately
  • Essay subjects: Structure answers with clear points and examples
